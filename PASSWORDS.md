# Secret passwords ()

All 45 entries of `data_general/secret_pass.dat`, **in English since 2026-09-24**
(`tools/passwords.py`, installed by `build_patch.py`). The Japanese passwords
no longer work in the patched game; they are kept below for reference.

**Typing them:** capital letters on the ABC page (the keyboard opens there), one
line per row of the password screen, no spaces -- at most 9 letters a line. The
game compares the lines exactly. Enter them from the title screen's Password
option, or in-game at the secret-spell fairy (`ev190000`); rewards arrive in the
mailbox, and each works once per save.

None of them is shown in-game: the originals were printed in magazines, given
out at events and posted on the Square Enix site, so the release notes should
carry this list.
